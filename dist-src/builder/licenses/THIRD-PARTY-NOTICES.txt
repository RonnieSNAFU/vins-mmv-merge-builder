Shipped next to the exe:
- Res\HavokTypeRegistry*.xml: HKLib's Havok type registry (MIT, HKLib-MIT.txt).
- data\smithbox\: Smithbox param definitions, Param Meta, Param Type Info and the Nightreign TAE template
  (MIT, Smithbox-MIT.txt). This is community reverse-engineering metadata, not game data.
- data\andre\: Andre's archive file-name dictionaries for Elden Ring and Nightreign (MIT, Smithbox-MIT.txt).
- data\*.json, data\*.tsv, data\merge\, data\templates\: ours (GPL-3.0). The manifests hold only file names, sizes
  and SHA-256 hashes; the merge rules hold which side wins per AI function plus 5 short lines.

NOT shipped (taken from your PC or downloaded at build time, checked by SHA-256):
- Elden Vins Nightreign and More Map Variations 2.1.8-hotfix3 & Weapons: you download them from their Nexus pages.
- Nightreign and Elden Ring game files and Oodle (oo2core_*.dll): read from your Steam installs.
- Nightreign 1.03.4 regulation.bin (vawser/Smithbox), c0000.hks base (El-Fonz0/EldenRingNightreignHKS) and
  nr-common.emedf.json (AinTunez/DarkScript3): downloaded from GitHub at pinned commits (see data\fetch.json).
- Mod Engine 3 (garyttierney/me3): the builder offers to download its official installer if you do not have it.
